What it does

  • Reminds you by mileage and date, with intervals from the manufacturer's owner's manual (page cited) for covered models, or your own for anything else.
  • Unit number, plate and tire size for each vehicle.
  • A printable maintenance record sheet per vehicle: date, mileage, service, parts, who did it.
  • One CSV of every vehicle with last service, next due and status.
  • A driver photographs the odometer and shares the photo, unit and reading with you through the phone's share sheet.
  • Logs repairs and part replacements, tracks registration, insurance and inspection dates.

What it doesn't do

  • No GPS or location tracking.
  • No accounts and no shared dashboard. Each phone keeps its own records.

Your data

Everything stays on the phone. Nothing is uploaded, and you can back it up to a file.
